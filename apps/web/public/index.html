    § 02 four axes · two polarities

    What the score measures

    Four axes, two polarities. Design and copy measure slop, so lower is better. System and AEO measure fit and reach, so higher is better.

    design slop
    Slop score · lower is better

    27 weighted CSS and DOM patterns: the reflex fonts, purple CTAs, gradient headlines and glassmorphic cards generated pages converge on.

    copy slop
    Slop score · lower is better

    Prose tells on the words: LLM buzzword clusters, em-dash overuse, exclamation inflation and "not just X, it's Y" antithesis framing.

    system · DESIGN.md
    higher is better

    Token compliance against a site's own declared DESIGN.md fonts, palette and corner radii so bespoke brands never false-positive.

    aeo · agent-readable
    higher is better

    Deterministic checks verifying whether ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can fetch, parse and cite the page via robots.txt, llms.txt and Markdown twins.

    continuity

    Slop is the hook. Staying on-system is the product.

    Sites do not become slop in one commit. A teammate ships a section, an agent "improves" a hero, and six weeks later the page reads like everyone else's.

    Monitoring opens with a waitlist: join now and your baseline is recorded today. At launch it re-scans your domain daily and emails you when it slips; publish a DESIGN.md and every sweep also checks the page against your own declared tokens, naming exactly what drifted.

    ↻
    Daily re-scan

    At launch: re-scans every monitored domain each day and emails you the moment the slop score regresses. Waitlist baselines are already being recorded.

    ◳
    DESIGN.md drift

    Checks each scan against your declared tokens and names the drift: a font nobody approved, a CTA off the palette.

    ⎙
    Client report

    Every monitored domain gets a print-ready report at /report/<domain> to hand a client or a team.

    ⊘
    Double opt-in

    Nothing is emailed until you confirm. Your address is stored only to send the alerts you asked for; one request deletes it.

    Dashboard & directory
